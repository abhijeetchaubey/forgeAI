ACME CORP EMPLOYEE BENEFITS GUIDE

1. Medical, Dental, and Vision Insurance:
ACME Corp covers 90% of monthly medical insurance premiums for employees and
75% for eligible dependents. Preventive dental and vision care are covered at 100%.

2. Retirement 401(k) Plan:
The company provides a 100% dollar-for-dollar 401(k) match up to 5% of base
annual salary with immediate vesting from day one.

3. Learning & Wellness Stipends:
- Annual Learning & Development Stipend: $1,500 USD per year for courses, books, or conferences.
- Annual Wellness Reimbursement: $600 USD per year for gym memberships or fitness equipment.
