ACME CORP OFFICIAL LEAVE POLICY

1. Annual Paid Time Off (PTO):
Full-time employees accrue 22 days of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year.
A maximum of 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

2. Paid Sick Leave:
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Absence exceeding 3 consecutive
working days requires a licensed physician's medical certificate.

3. Parental Leave:
All eligible parents (primary and secondary caregivers) receive 16 weeks of
100% paid parental leave following birth or adoption.

4. Bereavement Leave:
Employees are granted 5 paid days of bereavement leave for immediate family.
