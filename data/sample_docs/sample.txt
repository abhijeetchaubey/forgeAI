ForgeAI Factory Operational Manual: Safety Protocols & Robotic Cell Operations.

Chapter 1: Overview and Architecture
The automated manufacturing floor consists of three high-precision robotic workcells.
Each cell is continuously monitored by IoT telemetry sensors reporting pressure, vibration, and temperature.
Standard operating procedures require daily calibration of all pneumatic actuators and weekly inspection of safety interlocks.

Chapter 2: Thermal Management and Emergency Cutoff
In the event of an anomalous temperature spike exceeding 85 degrees Celsius, automated shutoff triggers immediately.
Operators must inspect the auxiliary cooling loop and ensure coolant valve clearance before manual restart.
All shutdown events must be logged into the central factory ledger with telemetry snapshots.
